Lord Krishna

Lord Krishna is one of the most revered and beloved deities in Hinduism. Known as the eighth incarnation of Lord Vishnu, he is celebrated for his wisdom, charm, and divine teachings.

His life and teachings are beautifully depicted in the Mahabharata, especially in the Bhagavad Gita, where he imparts profound spiritual guidance to Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.

Krishna is also remembered for his playful childhood stories in Vrindavan, his divine flute music, and his role as a protector of dharma.

  • Krishna is often depicted with a peacock feather in his crown.
  • He is known for lifting the Govardhan Hill to protect his devotees.
  • Janmashtami is celebrated as his birth anniversary.
  • His teachings emphasize devotion, selflessness, and righteousness.
  • Krishna's Ras Leela with the Gopis symbolizes divine love.
